# V11.39.8 — Regime & Multi-Timeframe Confirmation

- Freezes weekly, daily and hourly trend confirmation for each current opportunity.
- Measures relative strength versus SPY and the appropriate sector ETF.
- Adds market breadth and market-regime context.
- Scores breakout quality from prior levels, relative volume, close location and ATR extension.
- Records explicit contradictions and structural invalidation guidance.
- Fails closed when required hourly or breakout evidence is unavailable.
- Makes multi-timeframe confirmation a required gate before a virtual Shadow Book entry.
- Adds the evidence to Investment Desk, Discord and the persistent audit record.

The system remains research-only. It cannot modify its structural rules, connect
to a broker or transmit orders. No profitability or backtest result is claimed.
